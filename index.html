Questions before you start?

Ask a question on WhatsApp
Will my team need another app?

No. Your staff message Tusker using the WhatsApp they already have. They can record enquiries, add notes and update progress there.

Can Tusker follow our own sales process?

The stages and information collected can be configured around your process. For example: new enquiry, contacted, site visited, quote sent and won. You review the setup before your team starts. More involved processes can be scoped through managed setup.

Can my team use voice notes?

Yes. They can speak an enquiry, note or update into WhatsApp. Tusker asks for required details and confirmation before saving a change.

Will Tusker automatically collect all our enquiries?

In the setup shown here, your team records enquiries by messaging Tusker. It asks for the details needed to create the record. Updates go into the same enquiry as work moves forward.

What can I see as the business owner?

You can ask which enquiries are still new, check the stage of an enquiry and read the team’s notes. WhatsApp notifications can be configured for important updates, such as a site visit completed or a quote sent.

Will Tusker call customers or send quotations for us?

Your team handles customer conversations, visits and quotations. Tusker keeps their updates together so you can see what needs attention. Customers continue contacting your business as they do today; Tusker’s number is for your staff.

Does Tusker read our other WhatsApp chats?

Tusker works with the messages your team sends to its WhatsApp number. Your staff record the enquiries and updates they want to track there. Your other customer conversations continue as usual.

What is the difference between self-serve and managed setup?

With self-serve, get up and running in 15 minutes with one standard enquiry process. Tusker guides you on WhatsApp. No developers needed, no complex integrations and no setup charge. Available on Starter and Team.

With managed setup, use our consulting experience to review and simplify your enquiry process. We configure the agreed stages and owner updates, help onboard your team, and include one training session and one round of adjustments. A one-time setup fee applies alongside your monthly plan.

How much will Tusker cost my business?

Starter is ₹1,499 a month for up to 5 people. Team is ₹2,499 a month for up to 15 people. Custom is available only with managed setup and starts at ₹4,999 a month. The owner counts as a team member.

Starter and Team have no setup charge when you choose self-serve. Optional managed setup is ₹2,999 for Starter and ₹4,999 for Team. Custom requires managed setup, starting at ₹14,999 once. Applicable taxes are extra. Compare the plans and first-month totals.

Can we try it before a full rollout?

Yes. Start with a 14-day trial of one standard process for up to 5 people. Use it to check how your team records enquiries and updates. Choose a paid plan and any managed setup once you are ready to roll it out.
